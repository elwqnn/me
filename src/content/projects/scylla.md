[Scylla](https://scylla-ops.com) is an [open-source](https://github.com/scylla-ops/scylla) CI/CD platform, written entirely in Rust. The idea is simple: pipelines that are quick to set up and easy to read, whether you're a solo developer or a whole team.

We're a team of six, and the beta has been open since May. Since 0.5, you can [try it right in your browser](https://app.scylla-ops.com), with nothing to install. On my side, I mostly work on the back-end, and I also designed the brand identity and guidelines you see below.
